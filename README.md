The Sluice Gate — A Poem About Stopping. A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. At a sawah sluice gate, a daughter says stop; her father, hand on the board his own father taught him, does not lift it — he sits in the mud beside her, and the water she kept holds the whole field still. The program writes the poem to entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
